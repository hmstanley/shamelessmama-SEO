## The Three Things Holding You Back

Despite all the above, three fixable issues are limiting how high you rank — especially in Google (vs. Bing) and in AI tools like ChatGPT.

### 🔴 Issue 1 — Google doesn't know where you are

Your website's behind-the-scenes address information lists **Los Angeles** — but you serve San Francisco, Sacramento, and the Bay Area. Google uses this hidden address to decide who shows up in local searches. When someone in SF searches for you, Google isn't sure you're really there.

**What this costs you:** You may be invisible in Google Maps results and "near me" searches for San Francisco.

**How hard to fix:** Easy. 30 minutes, no tech skills needed.

---

### 🔴 Issue 2 — You don't have a Google Business Profile

Google Business Profile is the **free listing** that shows your name, photo, reviews, phone number, and a map pin when someone searches for you directly. It's what makes you show up in the box with the map. Right now, that box goes to other therapists.

**What this costs you:** Every person who searches "postpartum therapist near me" on Google sees a map with your competitors — not you.

**How hard to fix:** Easy. About 1 hour to set up, free.

---

### 🟡 Issue 3 — ChatGPT and AI tools don't know enough about you

When someone asks ChatGPT "who is a good birth trauma therapist in San Francisco," it pulls answers from directories like Psychology Today and Healthgrades. If your profile on those sites isn't complete and linked to your website, you won't come up.

**What this costs you:** As more people use AI to find therapists, you miss those referrals.

**How hard to fix:** Medium. A few hours spread across a couple of afternoons.

Once these three things are corrected:
