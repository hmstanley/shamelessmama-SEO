## Fix 1 — Correct Your Hidden Address on Your Website
**Time needed: 30 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐ Easy**

Your Squarespace website has a hidden "business information" section that tells Google where you are. It currently says Los Angeles. We need it to say San Francisco (or your primary service city).

### Steps:

**1. Log into Squarespace**
- Go to [squarespace.com](https://squarespace.com) and sign in
- Click on your website to open it

**2. Go to Settings**
- In the left sidebar, click **Settings**
- Then click **Business Information**

**3. Update your address**
- Change the address to your San Francisco address (or if you work fully online, use: San Francisco Bay Area, CA)
- Make sure the city says **San Francisco** (not Los Angeles)
- Save

**4. While you're there — fill in these fields if they're blank:**
- Business name: Shameless Mama Wellness
- Phone: 415.320.6842
- Email: marilyn@shamelessmamawellness.com

**5. Save and you're done ✓**

> 💡 Why this matters: Google reads this hidden information to decide if you're a San Francisco business. Right now it thinks you're in LA, so it may not show you to people searching in SF.

## Fix 2 — Set Up Your Google Business Profile
**Time needed: 1–2 hours (spread over a few days for verification) | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Easy-Medium**

This is the single most impactful thing you can do. It's completely free and makes you show up on Google Maps.

### Steps:

**1. Go to Google Business Profile**
- Open your browser and go to: [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
- Sign in with your Google account (Gmail)

**2. Search for your business**
- Type "Shameless Mama Wellness" in the search box
- If it appears, click **Claim this business** — Google may already know you exist
- If it doesn't appear, click **Add your business**

**3. Fill in your business details**

When it asks, enter:
- **Business name:** Shameless Mama Wellness
- **Category:** Mental Health Therapist *(search for this — it's in the dropdown)*
- **Do you have a physical location customers visit?** → Select **No** (since you're online)
- **Service area:** Add San Francisco, Sacramento, Los Angeles, and California
- **Phone:** 415.320.6842
- **Website:** https://www.shamelessmamawellness.com

**4. Verify your business**
- Google will need to confirm you're real. They'll offer to call you or send a postcard
- **Choose the phone call** — it's instant
- They'll call and give you a 6-digit code. Type it in.

**5. Complete your profile — this part matters a lot**

Once verified, fill in as much as possible:
- **Description** — copy and paste this (or write your own):
  > "Marilyn Coleman, LCSW PMH-C, is a Postpartum and Birth Trauma Therapist serving the San Francisco Bay Area, Sacramento, and Los Angeles through online therapy. Specializing in EMDR, perinatal mental health, postpartum depression, postpartum anxiety, and birth trauma."
- **Hours:** Set your typical availability (even if you do consultations by appointment)
- **Photos:** Upload your headshot and logo
- **Services:** Add Postpartum Therapy, Birth Trauma Therapy, EMDR, Prenatal Therapy, etc.

**6. Ask your first clients for reviews**
- Once your profile is live, you'll get a link to share
- Send it to 2–3 past clients you trust and ask if they'd leave a review
- Reviews are the #1 thing that moves you up in Google Maps

> 💡 Why this matters: Google Maps results appear ABOVE regular search results. Right now other therapists are in that map box — you're not. This gets you in the box.

## Fix 3 — Strengthen Your Psychology Today Profile
**Time needed: 45 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐ Easy**

Psychology Today is the directory Google and ChatGPT trust most for finding therapists. If your profile there is incomplete or doesn't link to your website, it hurts you.

### Steps:

**1. Log into your Psychology Today profile**
- Go to [therapists.psychologytoday.com](https://therapists.psychologytoday.com) and sign in

**2. Check these things:**

✅ **Website field** — make sure it says: https://www.shamelessmamawellness.com
- This link tells Google that your website and your Psychology Today profile are the same person

✅ **Specialties** — make sure these are listed:
- Postpartum Depression
- Postpartum Anxiety
- Birth Trauma
- EMDR
- Perinatal Mental Health
- Pregnancy, Prenatal, Postpartum

✅ **Location/service area** — make sure it shows:
- San Francisco Bay Area
- Sacramento
- Los Angeles
- Online (California)

✅ **Photo** — use the same professional headshot as your website and Google Business Profile

✅ **Bio** — if it's short, expand it. Use the words "birth trauma," "postpartum," "EMDR," and "San Francisco" naturally in your bio text.

**3. Save everything and you're done ✓**

> 💡 Why this matters: When someone asks ChatGPT "find me a birth trauma therapist in San Francisco," it reads Psychology Today. If your profile is complete with a website link, you're more likely to be recommended.

## Fix 4 — Add Yourself to Two More Directories
**Time needed: 30 minutes each | Difficulty: ⭐ Easy**

These two directories are free and trusted by both Google and AI tools:

### Healthgrades
- Go to: [healthgrades.com/registration/clinician](https://www.healthgrades.com/registration/clinician)

- Fill in the same information as your Psychology Today profile
- Add your website link

### Zencare
- Go to: [zencare.co/for-therapists](https://zencare.co/for-therapists)
- Zencare specializes in therapists and ranks well in AI searches
- Fill in your specialties and website

> 💡 Why this matters: Every trusted website that lists your name AND links to shamelessmamawellness.com tells Google "this therapist is real and authoritative." The more of these you have, the higher you rank.

## Fix 5 — Keep Doing What's Already Working
**Time needed: 1–2 hours per blog post | Difficulty: ⭐ Easy (you're already doing it)**

Your blog is your biggest SEO asset. 79 posts is exceptional for a solo practice. Keep publishing. A few tips to make each post work even harder:

**Before you publish each blog post, check:**

✅ Does the post title include a keyword someone would search?
- Good: "What Is Birth Trauma? Signs You Might Be Overlooking"
- Less good: "My Thoughts on Difficult Births"

✅ Does the first paragraph include San Francisco, Bay Area, or California?
- Example: "As a birth trauma therapist in the San Francisco Bay Area..."

✅ Is there a link to one of your service pages?
- Link the words "birth trauma therapy" to your `/traumatic-childbirth-therapist-sacramento` page

✅ Is there a call-to-action at the end?
- "Ready to talk? Book a free consultation →"
